## Skills
frontend-design, dataviz.

## Steps
1. Build the homepage exactly as in 9.0 to 9.3: hero (the question in display type plus the How to read a tile panel), the 12 headline tiles in four groups with varied spans, the comparison section (income vs life expectancy scatter, GDP per capita growth lines, the largest-gap panel with its stated rule), the three area cards with snapshot sentences, and the footer.
2. Snapshot sentences come from fixed templates filled with real values in both languages (07-language-writing.md).
3. Implement the one orchestrated motion: the delta contours draw in once on first visit, skipped for reduced motion and slow devices.
4. Check the performance budget with the glass, grain and contours enabled on a mid-range phone profile.

## Done when
- Every tile and chart has a working source line.
- No tile or chart appears for data that failed verification.
- Both languages complete; budget met.

## Then
Run tests, update docs/PROGRESS.md, commit, and stop. Report: what was built, three things for the user to check by eye, anything unresolved.
